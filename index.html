Reported research scope

5,104 NFL games from 2000 through 2019. Pregame features included home-team identity, game week, primetime (kickoff at or after 7 PM), and a late-season indicator (weeks 13–17). The 2019 season was reserved for final testing.

Home-team identity was the most influential feature among those available. Feature importance describes model reliance and does not establish a causal effect.

Verified in supplied research brief

Forecasting NFL Game Attendance

Original project summary: 5,104 NFL games, 2000–2019. Compared Linear Regression, Ridge Regression, and Random Forest with cross-validated MAE of 4,401, 4,344, and 3,045 fans respectively. 2019 was held for final testing.

Poster title supplied by project owner: Forecasting NFL Game Attendance. The poster file itself was not present in the inspected workspace.

Study finding

What the best score means

Random Forest’s MAE was 1,356 fans lower than Linear Regression, approximately 30.8% lower relative to the Linear Regression MAE. MAE is an average absolute error, not a guarantee or interval for one game.

Scope boundary

Attendance is not utilization

The supplied research forecast attendance from pregame information. It does not provide paired ticket-sales and entry-scan records or a validated no-show model. Castella’s simulator applies only the utilization rate you choose.

Not available

Sources still needed

The original poster file, modeling notebook, trained estimator, and validation predictions were not found in the inspected workspace. Supplemental historical reported attendance is available in Analytics. No NFL-wide utilization rate is asserted.
