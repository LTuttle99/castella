    Average absolute error

    Lower is better. Reported cross-validation results from the original study, not new model evaluation.

    Reported research scope

    5,104 NFL games from 2000 through 2019. Pregame features included home-team identity, game week, primetime (kickoff at or after 7 PM), and a late-season indicator (weeks 13–17). The 2019 season was reserved for final testing.

    Home-team identity was the most influential feature among those available. Feature importance describes model reliance and does not establish a causal effect.
    Verified in supplied research brief

    Forecasting NFL Game Attendance

    Original project summary: 5,104 NFL games, 2000–2019. Linear Regression, Ridge Regression and Random Forest had cross-validated MAE of 4,401, 4,344 and 3,045 fans. 2019 was held for final testing.

    Poster title supplied by project owner. The poster file itself was not present in the inspected workspace.

    Study finding

    What the best score means

    Random Forest's MAE was 1,356 fans lower than Linear Regression, about 30.8% lower. MAE is an average absolute error, not a guarantee or interval for one game.

    Scope boundary

    Attendance is not utilization

    The research forecast attendance from pregame information. It provides no paired ticket-sales and entry-scan records or validated no-show model. Ticket scenarios apply only the rate you choose.

    Not available

    Sources still needed

    The original poster file, modeling notebook, trained estimator and validation predictions were not found. Castella does not forecast future games.

    Privacy & data

    Chat stays in page memory and is cleared on reload. Nothing is sent to an external API. Browser AI runs in your browser; Ollama runs on your machine, only after you connect.

    Saved locally per profile: personality, assistant mode, saved scenarios, achievements and celebration history, in this browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes them.

    Data definitions. Reported attendance is source-reported and not gate scans. Tickets sold or distributed are your inputs. Illustrative arrivals are tickets × your assumed show-up rate. Castella labels each figure as a verified observation, published benchmark, derived estimate, user assumption or illustrative placeholder. No forecasting model is connected.
