# Castella handoff

Castella is NFL Attendance Intelligence & Stadium Operations Assistant, a no-login browser app that explains the supplied NFL attendance research and helps a stadium planner explore what different ticket-utilization assumptions imply for arrivals. Its deterministic assistant handles research questions and multi-turn ticket calculations, distinguishes no-show from show-up rates, retains previous scenarios, compares changes, offers professional, balanced, and fun tones, and can transfer a calculated scenario into the simulator. It also calculates illustrative staffing, concession, parking, and entry demand from editable assumptions.

The assistant uses local retrieval and deterministic response templates, not generative AI. No LLM credentials or backend are configured. The public schedule importer is implemented, but live retrieval failed in this environment because DNS/network access was unavailable. No public historical data is bundled and no historical chart is shown. The original trained research model and underlying data were not supplied, so the app cannot forecast an upcoming game. Scenario rates and operations ratios are user assumptions, not established league standards. Legacy local browser data was migrated to Castella storage keys on the same origin. The app was opened and interacted with in a browser at localhost. Static production output can be created with `python3 scripts/build.py`. It is prepared for static hosting, but has not been publicly deployed.
