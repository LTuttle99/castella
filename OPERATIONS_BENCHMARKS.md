Editing a selected source wage reclassifies it as custom.

## Reference-only records

MSFA financial statement, FY2024 (report published January 2025): total operating expenses USD 96,927,811, stadium operating expenses USD 49,479,796, stadium operating revenue USD 43,832,603. Units are USD/fiscal-year, authority-wide scope, not per NFL game or fan. Total expense includes noncash depreciation/amortization. No revenue figure is relabeled concession gross sales or profit.

Lucas Oil Stadium’s approximate football seating reference is 67,000 seats. SGSA’s UK ingress reference is 660 people/entry point/hour, subject to measured lower rates. Both pages are undated and both values are reference-only; neither populates current event capacity or NFL processing rates automatically. Exact URLs and limitations are in OPERATIONS_RESEARCH.md and each record.

## Unavailable defaults

Absolute concession spending per attendee, empirical universal NFL staff ratios, measured local mode shares/vehicle occupancy, measured NFL entry/security throughput and Indianapolis occupational wages remain unavailable in this collection. Their research modes are disabled with an explanation. User-entered spending estimates represent hypothetical gross food/beverage spending only, not profit or retained revenue.
